Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about treatments, fees, and visits.

Is Root Canal Treatment (RCT) really painless here? ▼
Yes! With localized computer-guided anesthesia, rotary apex locators, and motorized instruments, modern RCTs at our clinic are virtually painless and typically completed in 1 to 2 sittings.
How long do dental implants last? ▼
With regular brushing, flossing, and periodic cleanings, medical-grade titanium implants can last 20 years to a lifetime. They fuse directly with your jawbone, behaving like natural roots.
Where is the clinic located in Ajmer? ▼
We are conveniently located at E 29, Shastri Nagar, right opposite Pashupalan Vibhag in Ajmer, Rajasthan, with convenient parking and accessibility.
Do I need an appointment or can I walk in? ▼
Walk-ins and dental emergencies are always attended to promptly. However, scheduling in advance ensures zero waiting time with our prosthodontic and dental specialists.
